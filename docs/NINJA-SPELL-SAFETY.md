## Remaining work

These gates do not provide SQL transactions, crash-safe spell-file replacement,
whole proficiency/XP transactions, arbitrary raw-writer exclusion or cross-reader
atomic snapshots. IO can leave a partial save; postcommit notification can fail,
and concurrent notification order is unproved. Source rental retention is separate
debt. XP range checks apply when progress is actually prepared, not universally to
capped/rune/level-gated paths. Count-based progression, previous-level restoration,
unused/next-level metadata rules and native owner lifecycle remain open.

Outer HundredWeapons mutation/load and rank/info prefixes, multi-item Pirate
skills, AirPower spending and later Status calculations remain incomplete. Safe
child APIs do not make those outer transactions complete.

Ordinary admission still needs native class hydration, real Ninja/spell/equipment
owners, metadata/rank lifecycle and a real sender. Session spell snapshots and native
spell maps are distinct; a test queue or Fake mode cannot bridge them. Next is
prepared saved-owner hydration and Store/connection-bound startup publication,
then separately evidenced request/economy adoption. No live client acceptance,
full English, distribution, deployment, full parity or all-bugs-fixed claim follows.
Original saves, routes, restore copies and running sessions remain preserved.
