pins Store, connection, actual holder identity, account/UID, physical client and
Player, and constructor-bound SpellStorage. Admission prepares detached storage,

binding/current physical owners. Removal retires it; same-UID recreation does not

Prepared mailbox package publication retains spells. Its sole actual producer

A successful durable writer followed by failed plain publication proof is a
committed-publication failure. Retire/fence the actual owner and surface that
